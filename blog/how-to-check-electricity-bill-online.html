How To Check Electricity Bill Online in Pakistan

January 1, 2025 Complete Guide 12 min read

Checking your electricity bill online is a convenient way to view your monthly electricity charges without visiting an electricity office or waiting for a printed bill. Consumers in Pakistan can generally access their electricity bills through their electricity distribution company's official website using a reference number or customer ID.

Whether your electricity provider is LESCO, IESCO, FESCO, MEPCO, GEPCO, PESCO, or another distribution company, the online bill-checking process is similar. You need to identify your electricity provider, enter the required billing information, and view your latest bill.

This guide explains how to check your electricity bill online in Pakistan, find your reference number, download a duplicate bill, and troubleshoot common problems.

Quick Tip: Keep a previous electricity bill handy before starting — it contains your reference number or customer ID required to retrieve your billing information.

How to Check Your Electricity Bill Online

You can check your electricity bill online by following a few simple steps. Before starting, keep a previous electricity bill available because it usually contains the reference number or customer ID required to retrieve your billing information.

Step 1: Identify Your Electricity Provider

First, identify the company responsible for supplying electricity to your area. Pakistan has several electricity distribution companies, and each generally provides its own online billing service.

Some major electricity providers include:

  • LESCO – Lahore Electric Supply Company
  • IESCO – Islamabad Electric Supply Company
  • FESCO – Faisalabad Electric Supply Company
  • MEPCO – Multan Electric Power Company
  • GEPCO – Gujranwala Electric Power Company
  • PESCO – Peshawar Electric Supply Company
  • HESCO – Hyderabad Electric Supply Company
  • SEPCO – Sukkur Electric Power Company
  • QESCO – Quetta Electric Supply Company
  • TESCO – Tribal Electric Supply Company
  • K-Electric – Karachi's electricity utility

Choose the company shown on your previous electricity bill. Selecting the correct provider is important because your billing information may not appear on another company's website.

Step 2: Find Your Electricity Bill Reference Number

Most electricity distribution companies use a reference number to identify an electricity connection and retrieve its billing details.

You can usually find this number on a previous electricity bill. Look near the upper portion of the bill, where customer and connection information is displayed.

A common reference number format used by many Pakistani distribution companies contains 14 digits. However, the exact information required can vary by provider.

Some companies also offer bill lookup through a customer ID.

Make sure you enter the number correctly. Even a small typing mistake may prevent the system from finding your bill.

Step 3: Open Your Electricity Provider's Official Website

Visit the official website of your electricity distribution company.

Look for an option such as:

  • Online Bill
  • Duplicate Bill
  • Bill Information
  • View Electricity Bill

The exact wording and location of this option may differ between providers.

For safety, use your electricity company's official website rather than entering billing information on an unfamiliar website.

Step 4: Enter Your Reference Number or Customer ID

Open the online bill-checking section and enter the requested information.

Depending on your electricity provider, you may need to provide:

  • Reference number
  • Customer ID
  • Account number

Enter the details exactly as printed on your previous electricity bill. If the website asks for a specific number format, follow its instructions.

Step 5: View Your Electricity Bill

After submitting the required information, the website should display your available billing details if the record is available.

Review the displayed information to confirm that it belongs to your electricity connection.

You may be able to see:

  • Consumer information
  • Billing month
  • Electricity units consumed
  • Current charges
  • Previous outstanding balance
  • Total amount payable
  • Payment due date

The information displayed depends on the electricity provider's billing system.

How to Find Your Electricity Bill Reference Number

Your electricity bill reference number is an important identifier used by many electricity providers to retrieve billing records.

If you want to check your bill online but do not know your reference number, follow these suggestions.

1. Check a previous electricity bill

Look near the upper section of your previous bill. The reference number is generally displayed with the consumer and connection details.

2. Look for your customer information

Some electricity providers display a separate customer ID or account number. Check which identifier their online billing portal accepts.

3. Contact your electricity provider

If you have lost your previous bill and cannot find your reference number, contact your electricity distribution company's customer support or visit an authorized customer service office.

You may need to provide information to help them identify your electricity connection.

How to Download a Duplicate Electricity Bill

A duplicate electricity bill is an electronic copy of an existing bill that you can view, download, or print.

It is useful when:

  • Your printed bill has not arrived.
  • You have misplaced your electricity bill.
  • You need another copy for your records.
  • You want to review your bill before its due date.
  • You need a copy for payment or personal documentation.

To download a duplicate electricity bill:

  1. Visit your electricity provider's official online billing portal.
  2. Enter your reference number, customer ID, or other required identifier.
  3. Submit the information.
  4. Open the displayed bill.
  5. Look for a Download, Print, or PDF option.
  6. Save the available document to your device.

If the website displays the bill in a browser window without a download button, you may be able to use your browser's print function and select Save as PDF, provided the bill is displayed correctly.

Keep the downloaded bill somewhere accessible so you can review it when needed.

How to Check an Electricity Bill Without a Reference Number

Many Pakistani electricity billing portals require a reference number or customer ID to retrieve a bill.

If you do not have your reference number, try the following options:

  • Check an older electricity bill.
  • Look for an electronic copy of a previous bill.
  • Check whether your electricity provider supports customer ID-based lookup.
  • Contact the electricity company's customer service department.
  • Visit an authorized customer service center if online identification is unavailable.

Some providers may support different lookup methods, while others require a specific connection identifier.

Warning: Do not enter random numbers or personal information into unofficial websites claiming to retrieve your electricity bill.

What Information Can You See on an Online Electricity Bill?

An online electricity bill generally contains several details about your electricity connection, consumption, and monthly charges.

Understanding these details can help you review your bill more effectively.

1. Consumer Information

This section identifies the electricity connection and may include the consumer's name, address, reference number, customer ID, or meter information.

Check these details to make sure the bill belongs to the correct connection.

2. Billing Month

The billing month identifies the period for which the electricity charges have been calculated.

It helps you understand which month's consumption is included in the bill.

3. Meter Reading

Meter reading information shows the electricity meter readings used to calculate consumption.

The bill may display previous and current readings, depending on the billing format.

4. Units Consumed

Electricity consumption is commonly measured in kilowatt-hours (kWh), also called electricity units.

Your bill uses the recorded consumption and applicable tariff structure to calculate electricity charges.

5. Current Charges

Current charges represent the charges associated with the billing period.

Depending on the applicable tariff and billing rules, these may include electricity charges and other applicable components.

6. Previous Balance

If an earlier amount remains unpaid, it may appear as a previous balance or outstanding amount.

Review this information carefully, especially if you have recently made a payment.

7. Due Date

The due date indicates the deadline for paying the bill under the displayed billing terms.

Check this date to avoid missing the payment deadline.

8. Total Amount Payable

This is the amount shown as payable according to the bill's calculation and payment status.

Always review the latest bill and any applicable payment instructions before making a payment.

Why Is Your Electricity Bill Not Showing Online?

Sometimes an online electricity bill does not appear even after entering the correct reference number or customer ID.

Several factors may explain this problem.

Incorrect Reference Number

A typing error can prevent the system from identifying your electricity connection.

Check the number printed on your previous bill and enter it again.

Wrong Electricity Provider

You may be using another company's online billing portal.

Confirm the provider's name on your electricity bill before trying again.

New Bill Has Not Been Uploaded

The latest bill may not yet be available online if the provider has not completed its billing update.

Try checking again later.

Website Maintenance or Technical Problems

An electricity company's website may temporarily become unavailable because of maintenance, technical issues, or heavy traffic.

Wait a while and try accessing the official website again.

Incorrect Customer ID

If the portal requires a customer ID but you enter a different identifier, the lookup may fail.

Check the required field and use the correct information.

Browser or Internet Connection Problems

A slow internet connection, outdated browser, or temporary browser issue can affect website functionality.

Try refreshing the page, checking your internet connection, or using another browser.

If the problem continues, contact your electricity provider for assistance.

How to Check Your Electricity Bill Due Date

You can generally find the payment due date by opening your latest electricity bill online.

After retrieving the bill:

  • Confirm that you have opened the latest billing month.
  • Find the due date section.
  • Review the total payable amount.
  • Check whether the bill includes any previous outstanding balance.
  • Follow the payment instructions provided by your electricity company.

Some electricity bills may display additional payment amounts or revised payment terms after the original due date. Review the latest available bill and applicable instructions before making a payment.

Is Checking an Electricity Bill Online Free?

Checking or viewing an electricity bill through an electricity provider's official online billing service is generally free.

Consumers can usually retrieve available bill information without paying a separate fee for simply viewing it.

However, payment services may have their own terms, processing arrangements, or charges depending on the payment provider.

Always distinguish between viewing an electricity bill and paying it.

Safety Tips When Checking Electricity Bills Online

Online bill-checking services are convenient, but you should still protect your information.

Follow these precautions:

  • Prefer your electricity provider's official website.
  • Double-check the website address before entering billing information.
  • Avoid suspicious websites that request unnecessary personal details.
  • Do not share banking passwords or one-time verification codes.
  • Use trusted payment applications and banking channels when paying bills.
  • Download bills only from sources you trust.
  • Avoid clicking unfamiliar links received through unsolicited messages.

Your reference number and other connection details should be handled carefully.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How can I check my electricity bill online in Pakistan?
Visit your electricity distribution company's online billing portal, enter the required reference number or customer ID, and submit the information to view your available bill.
2. Can I check my electricity bill using a reference number?
Yes. Many Pakistani electricity providers allow consumers to retrieve their bills using the reference number printed on a previous electricity bill.
3. How can I download my electricity bill in PDF format?
Open your latest bill through the provider's online portal and use the available download or print option. If supported by your browser, you can also save the displayed bill as a PDF.
4. Can I check my electricity bill without a previous bill?
You may be able to retrieve it using a customer ID or another identifier supported by your electricity provider. Otherwise, contact the provider to obtain your connection details.
5. Why is my electricity bill not showing online?
Possible reasons include an incorrect reference number, selecting the wrong provider, delayed bill uploading, website maintenance, or temporary technical problems.
6. Can I check my electricity bill on my mobile phone?
Yes. You can generally access your electricity provider's online billing portal through a mobile browser, provided the website supports mobile access.
7. Is an online duplicate electricity bill valid?
An electronic duplicate bill can be used to review billing details and, where supported, make payments through authorized channels. Specific requirements for printed copies or official documentation may vary.
8. How can I check my LESCO electricity bill online?
Select LESCO's official online billing service and enter the reference number or other required identifier printed on your previous LESCO bill.
9. How can I check my IESCO electricity bill online?
Use IESCO's official billing service, provide the required connection information, and retrieve the available electricity bill.
10. How can I check my K-Electric bill online?
Use K-Electric's official online customer billing service and enter the account information requested by its portal.
11. Can I check my electricity bill before receiving the printed copy?
Yes. If your electricity provider has uploaded the latest bill to its online system, you can generally view it before receiving the printed copy.
12. Is checking an electricity bill online the same as paying it?
No. Checking a bill allows you to view billing information. Paying a bill is a separate transaction that must be completed through an authorized payment channel.

Conclusion

Checking your electricity bill online in Pakistan is a convenient way to review monthly charges, check your due date, and download a duplicate bill when needed.

The most important step is identifying your electricity provider and using the correct reference number, customer ID, or account information. Always use the appropriate official billing portal and verify your bill details before making a payment.

By following these steps, you can access your available electricity billing information without depending entirely on a printed bill.
