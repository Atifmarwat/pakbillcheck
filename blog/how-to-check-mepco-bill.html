How to Check MEPCO Bill Online – Complete Guide

January 1, 2025 MEPCO 10 min read

You can check your MEPCO electricity bill online using the reference number printed on your previous bill. The online bill service allows consumers to view their available billing information, check the payable amount and due date, and obtain a duplicate copy for printing or saving.

MEPCO stands for Multan Electric Power Company, an electricity distribution company in Pakistan. If your printed electricity bill has not arrived or you have misplaced it, checking the bill online can help you access the available billing information without waiting for another paper copy.

In this guide, you will learn how to check your MEPCO bill step by step, find your reference number, download a duplicate bill, and solve common problems with online bill checking.

Quick Tip: Always keep your previous electricity bill handy — it contains your 14-digit reference number which is required to check your bill online.

What Is MEPCO and Its Online Bill Service?

MEPCO stands for Multan Electric Power Company. It is responsible for electricity distribution in its designated service areas in Pakistan.

Like other electricity distribution companies, MEPCO consumers need their billing information to check monthly electricity charges, meter readings, payment deadlines, and outstanding amounts.

An online bill service provides a way to view available electricity billing information through a web browser. Instead of relying only on a printed bill, consumers can use the relevant connection information to retrieve an electronic copy.

An online duplicate bill is particularly useful when:

  • Your printed bill has not been delivered.
  • You have misplaced your electricity bill.
  • You want to check the amount before making a payment.
  • You need to confirm the current billing month.
  • You want to save a digital copy for future reference.

Where Can You Check Your MEPCO Bill Online?

You can start by visiting the commonly used PITC MEPCO online bill service:

  • MEPCO Bill Checking Page: https://bill.pitc.com.pk/mepcobill
  • MEPCO Official Website: https://mepco.com.pk/

The PITC page is commonly used to retrieve MEPCO electricity bills. The official MEPCO website is another starting point for company information and consumer services.

The availability and design of online services can change. If the direct bill page does not open, visit the official MEPCO website and look for its bill or consumer-service information.

Important: Always check the website address before entering consumer details.

What Do You Need to Check Your MEPCO Bill?

Before checking your bill, keep your previous electricity bill available if possible. It contains the information generally needed to identify your connection.

1. MEPCO Reference Number

The reference number is the main identifier commonly used to retrieve an electricity bill online.

It is printed on your previous MEPCO electricity bill. You should enter the complete number exactly as it appears.

A missing digit or typing error may prevent the service from finding the correct billing record.

2. Customer ID

Your electricity bill may also display a customer ID. This is a separate identifier from the reference number.

Some electricity bill services may display or request additional consumer information. Follow the instructions shown on the current online service rather than assuming that every field accepts the same number.

3. Internet Connection and a Device

You need an internet connection and a web browser to access the bill service. You can generally use:

  • An Android smartphone
  • An iPhone
  • A laptop
  • A desktop computer
  • A tablet

You do not need a specialized application just to open a bill webpage in a browser.

How to Check MEPCO Bill Online Step by Step

Follow these steps to view your available MEPCO electricity bill.

Step 1: Open the MEPCO Online Bill Service

Open Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or another browser on your device. Visit the commonly used PITC MEPCO bill page: https://bill.pitc.com.pk/mepcobill

Check the address carefully to make sure you have opened the intended service.

Step 2: Find Your Reference Number

Take your previous MEPCO electricity bill and locate the reference number. It is usually printed in the consumer or connection identification area of the bill.

Carefully copy the complete number. Do not confuse it with the customer ID or another number printed on the document.

Step 3: Enter Your Reference Number

Find the appropriate field on the online bill page and enter your reference number. If the service requests additional information, provide it according to the instructions displayed on the page.

Check the number again before continuing.

Step 4: Submit the Information

Select the available button to submit your information or view your bill. The service will attempt to retrieve the billing record associated with the details you entered.

Step 5: View Your MEPCO Electricity Bill

If your information is correct and the relevant bill is available, the service should display the bill details. Review the following information:

  • Consumer or connection details
  • Billing month
  • Meter reading information
  • Electricity units consumed
  • Current bill amount
  • Due date
  • Applicable charges and adjustments

Make sure the billing month and consumer information are correct before saving or printing the bill.

How to Find Your MEPCO Bill Reference Number

Your reference number is important because it helps identify the electricity connection associated with your bill.

If you do not know your reference number, check an older printed MEPCO electricity bill.

Where Is the Reference Number Printed?

Look in the consumer or connection identification section of your electricity bill. The reference number is generally displayed near the upper portion of the document.

Do not mistake it for:

  • Customer ID
  • Meter number
  • Account or consumer name
  • Other billing or payment numbers

These details may all appear on the same bill, but they serve different purposes.

What If You Do Not Have an Old Bill?

If you have misplaced your previous bill, try these options:

  • Check your phone or computer for a previously saved bill PDF.
  • Ask the person responsible for the electricity connection for a copy.
  • Check any old printed bill kept with your household documents.
  • Contact MEPCO through its official customer service channels and ask how to retrieve your connection information.

The company may require additional information to verify the connection. Do not assume that a consumer name or phone number alone will be sufficient.

How to Download MEPCO Duplicate Bill

A duplicate MEPCO bill is an additional copy of an electricity billing document. It is useful when the original paper bill is lost, damaged, or not delivered.

You can generally retrieve an available duplicate bill through the online bill service.

Steps to Download Your MEPCO Bill

  1. Open the PITC MEPCO bill service.
  2. Enter your complete reference number in the required field.
  3. Submit the information.
  4. Wait for the available bill details to appear.
  5. Look for the print or download option.
  6. Save the document if the service provides a PDF or printable version.

The exact options may depend on the current portal design. If a separate download button is not available, the browser's print function may allow you to save the displayed bill as a PDF.

How to Save MEPCO Bill as a PDF

If the bill opens in a printable browser page, you can often save it as a PDF.

On a Windows computer:

  1. Open the displayed bill.
  2. Press Ctrl + P.
  3. Open the printer or destination menu.
  4. Select Save as PDF, if available.
  5. Choose where to save the file.
  6. Select Save.

On a Mac:

  1. Open the bill.
  2. Press Command + P.
  3. Open the PDF option in the print window.
  4. Select Save as PDF, if available.
  5. Choose a folder and save the document.

On a mobile phone:

Open the browser's print, share, or document options and look for a PDF-saving feature. The exact process varies by device and browser.

How to Print MEPCO Bill Online

If you need a physical copy of your electricity bill, you can print the displayed document.

  1. Open the online MEPCO bill service.
  2. Enter your reference number.
  3. Retrieve the available bill.
  4. Open the print option or use your browser's print command.
  5. Check the print preview.
  6. Select your printer and print the document.

If you do not have a printer at home, save the bill as a PDF and take the file to a nearby printing shop.

Before printing, confirm that the billing month, reference number, and payable amount are visible and readable.

How to Check MEPCO Bill on a Mobile Phone

You do not need a desktop computer to check your MEPCO bill. A smartphone and an internet connection are generally enough to access the online bill service.

  1. Open Chrome or another browser on your phone.
  2. Visit https://bill.pitc.com.pk/mepcobill.
  3. Enter your reference number in the required field.
  4. Submit the information.
  5. View the available bill.
  6. Use your phone's available print, share, or save options if you need a copy.

If the bill appears too small, zoom in or rotate your phone to landscape mode.

You can save a PDF copy on your phone for later access, provided the portal or browser offers that option.

How to Check MEPCO Bill Due Date and Amount

After opening your online bill, review its payment information carefully. Two of the most important details are the due date and the amount payable.

MEPCO Bill Due Date

The due date is the payment deadline displayed on the electricity bill. Check the date printed on your latest bill rather than using the date from a previous month. Payment deadlines may differ between billing periods.

MEPCO Bill Amount

The payable amount is the amount shown on the bill for the relevant billing period. Depending on the document, you may see different payment amounts or instructions for payment before and after the due date.

Read the current bill carefully to understand which amount applies and when it must be paid.

Why Is My MEPCO Bill Not Showing Online?

Sometimes a consumer enters a reference number but cannot view the expected bill. There can be several reasons for this problem.

1. Incorrect Reference Number

A missing digit or typing error can prevent the service from locating the correct electricity connection.

What to do: Compare the entered number with the reference number printed on your previous bill. Enter the complete number again.

2. Latest Bill Is Not Available Yet

The latest bill may not appear immediately if the relevant billing information has not yet been made available through the online service.

What to do: Try again later. If the delay continues, contact MEPCO through its official channels.

3. Temporary Website Problem

The online bill service may occasionally experience technical problems or temporary unavailability.

What to do: Refresh the page after some time or try accessing it in another browser.

4. Internet or Browser Issue

A slow internet connection, browser problem, or temporary loading issue may prevent the bill page from opening properly.

What to do:

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Refresh the webpage.
  • Close and reopen your browser.
  • Try another browser.
  • Clear the browser cache if necessary.

5. Additional Information Is Required

If the current service asks for more than one piece of information, entering only the reference number may not complete the lookup.

What to do: Follow the instructions displayed on the bill page and provide the requested information accurately.

If the problem continues after these checks, contact MEPCO through its official customer service channels.

Can You Check Your MEPCO Bill Without a Reference Number?

The reference number is generally the most convenient way to retrieve a MEPCO electricity bill online. If you do not have it, first check for an older printed bill or a previously saved digital copy.

You can also ask the person responsible for the electricity connection to provide the reference number.

If neither option is available, contact MEPCO and ask about its official procedure for identifying your connection. You may need to provide consumer details or complete a verification process.

Warning: Avoid relying on unofficial websites that promise to find your bill using incomplete information.

Tips for Safe and Accurate MEPCO Bill Checking

Follow these precautions when checking your electricity bill online:

  • Use the official MEPCO website or the recognized PITC bill service.
  • Check the website address before entering consumer information.
  • Enter the complete reference number accurately.
  • Verify the billing month before downloading or printing.
  • Review the payable amount and due date on the current bill.
  • Avoid websites that request unnecessary personal or financial information.
  • Do not share your consumer or billing details publicly.
  • Save a PDF copy if you need the document later.
  • Contact MEPCO through official channels if the bill information appears incorrect.

Remember: Viewing or downloading a duplicate bill does not automatically mean that the bill has been paid. Keep your payment receipt or verify the payment through an appropriate official payment channel.

Conclusion

Checking your MEPCO bill online is a convenient way to access your available electricity billing information without waiting for a printed copy. In most cases, you can use the reference number printed on a previous bill to retrieve the current available bill through the online service.

After opening the bill, review the billing month, consumer details, meter information, payable amount, and due date. If you need a physical copy, use the available print option or save the document as a PDF.

If your bill does not appear, check the reference number, try again later, and contact MEPCO through its official channels if the problem continues.
