How To Check LESCO Bill Online – Complete Guide

January 1, 2025 LESCO 10 min read

Checking your LESCO bill online is a convenient way to view your latest electricity bill without waiting for a printed copy. You can use the online bill service to view your bill details, check the due date, see the payable amount, and obtain a duplicate copy for printing.

To check your LESCO bill online, you generally need the reference number printed on a previous electricity bill. Open the online LESCO bill service, enter the required reference number, and submit it to view the available billing information.

This guide explains how to check your LESCO electricity bill step by step, find your reference number, download a duplicate bill, and troubleshoot common problems.

Quick Tip: Keep your previous LESCO electricity bill handy — it contains your reference number required to check your bill online.

Where Can You Check Your LESCO Bill Online?

LESCO stands for Lahore Electric Supply Company. It is an electricity distribution company responsible for supplying electricity to its designated service areas.

Consumers can use an online bill-viewing service to access their electricity billing information. The commonly used online electricity bill service is provided through the Pakistan Information Technology Company (PITC) platform.

You can start with these websites:

  • PITC LESCO bill service: https://bill.pitc.com.pk/lescobill
  • LESCO official website: https://lesco.gov.pk/

The PITC link is the direct LESCO bill page commonly used for viewing duplicate bills. If the page is unavailable or its address changes, visit the official LESCO website and look for its online bill or customer services section.

Important: Always check that you are using the correct website before entering your consumer information.

What Do You Need to Check Your LESCO Bill?

Before opening the online bill service, keep your previous electricity bill available if possible.

You may need the following information:

1. LESCO Reference Number

The reference number is the main information generally used to identify an electricity connection when checking a bill online.

It is printed on your previous LESCO electricity bill. Copy the complete number carefully, including all digits.

2. Customer ID

Your electricity bill may also contain a customer ID. Some online electricity services display or request additional consumer information, depending on the service and its current format.

Do not confuse your customer ID with your reference number. They are separate identifiers.

3. Internet Connection

You need an internet connection and a browser to open the online bill service. A smartphone, tablet, laptop, or desktop computer can be used.

How to Check LESCO Bill Online Step by Step

Follow these steps to view your latest available LESCO electricity bill.

Step 1: Open the LESCO Bill Checking Website

Open your browser, such as Chrome, Firefox, or Safari.

Visit the PITC LESCO bill page: https://bill.pitc.com.pk/lescobill

Make sure the website address is correct before entering any information.

Step 2: Find Your Reference Number

Take your previous LESCO electricity bill and locate the reference number.

It is usually printed in the consumer or bill identification section. Enter the complete number into the relevant field on the online bill page.

Do not enter an incomplete number or accidentally leave out digits.

Step 3: Enter the Required Information

Type your reference number into the field provided by the website.

If the page requests another identifier, such as a customer ID, follow the instructions displayed on the portal.

Check the information before submitting it.

Step 4: Submit Your Reference Number

Select the available button to submit or view your bill.

The online service will attempt to retrieve the billing record associated with the information you entered.

Step 5: View Your LESCO Electricity Bill

If the information is correct and the bill is available, the page should display the relevant bill details.

Review the information carefully, including:

  • Consumer name and connection details
  • Billing month
  • Meter reading information
  • Units consumed
  • Current bill amount
  • Due date
  • Any applicable additional charges or adjustments

The exact details displayed depend on the bill and the information available through the service.

Note: The portal's layout and available options may change. Follow the instructions currently displayed on the official service.

How to Find Your LESCO Bill Reference Number

Your reference number is important because it helps the online service identify your electricity connection.

If you do not know your reference number, check an older printed LESCO electricity bill.

Look for the section containing your consumer or connection identification information. The reference number is generally displayed prominently near the top portion of the bill.

Follow these steps:

  1. Find an old LESCO electricity bill.
  2. Look for the reference number field.
  3. Carefully identify the complete number.
  4. Copy it without missing any digits.
  5. Enter it into the online bill service.

What If You Do Not Have an Old Bill?

If you have lost your previous bill, try to obtain a copy from the person responsible for the electricity connection or check any saved digital copy.

You can also contact LESCO through its official customer service channels and ask how to retrieve your connection information. Be prepared to provide whatever consumer verification details the company requires.

Warning: Avoid using random third-party websites that claim they can find your reference number without proper verification.

How to Download LESCO Duplicate Bill

A duplicate LESCO bill is an additional copy of your electricity bill. It can be useful if your printed bill has been lost, damaged, or not delivered.

You can generally obtain a duplicate copy through the online bill service.

Steps to Download Your LESCO Bill

  1. Open the PITC LESCO bill service.
  2. Enter your complete reference number.
  3. Submit the required information.
  4. Wait for your bill details to appear.
  5. Look for the print or download option provided by the page.
  6. If a PDF is available, save it to your device.

The exact download method may depend on the current portal design. Some browser pages provide a direct print option rather than a separate PDF download button.

How to Save Your Bill as a PDF

If the bill opens in a printable browser page, you may be able to save it as a PDF.

On a computer:

  1. Open the bill.
  2. Press Ctrl + P on Windows or Command + P on Mac.
  3. Open the printer or destination options.
  4. Select Save as PDF, if available.
  5. Choose a folder.
  6. Save the file.

On a mobile phone, open the browser's print or share options and look for a PDF-saving option. The exact steps vary by device and browser.

How to Print LESCO Bill Online

If you need a physical copy of your electricity bill, you can print the displayed bill.

Follow these steps:

  1. Check your bill through the online service.
  2. Open the complete bill.
  3. Select the print option or use your browser's print command.
  4. Choose an available printer.
  5. Check the paper size and print preview.
  6. Select Print.

If you do not have a printer at home, save the bill as a PDF and take the file to a nearby printing shop.

Before printing, check that the bill is readable and that its reference number, billing month, and payable amount are visible.

How to Check LESCO Bill on a Mobile Phone

You do not need a computer to check your LESCO electricity bill. A smartphone with an internet connection and a web browser is generally sufficient.

Follow these steps:

  1. Open Chrome or another browser on your phone.
  2. Visit https://bill.pitc.com.pk/lescobill.
  3. Enter your reference number in the required field.
  4. Submit the information.
  5. View the available electricity bill.
  6. Use your phone's print, share, or save options if you need a copy.

If the bill is difficult to read, rotate your phone to landscape mode or zoom in on the relevant section.

You can also save a PDF copy on your phone so that you can access it later.

How to Check LESCO Bill Due Date and Amount

When your online bill appears, review the payment information before making a payment.

Two important details are the due date and the amount payable.

LESCO Bill Due Date

The due date is the payment deadline printed on the electricity bill.

Check the date shown on the current bill rather than relying on the date from a previous month's bill. Due dates can change from one billing cycle to another.

LESCO Bill Amount

The payable amount is the amount shown on the bill for the relevant payment period.

Depending on the bill, you may see separate amounts or payment details for different payment stages, including the amount due by the due date and any amount applicable after that date.

Read the payment instructions printed on the current bill carefully.

Why Is My LESCO Bill Not Showing Online?

Sometimes a consumer enters a reference number but cannot view the latest bill. This does not necessarily mean that the electricity connection has a problem.

Several reasons may explain why the bill is unavailable.

1. Incorrect Reference Number

A missing or incorrect digit can prevent the service from identifying the correct connection.

What to do: Compare the number you entered with the reference number on your previous bill. Re-enter it carefully.

2. The Latest Bill Is Not Available Yet

If the latest billing information has not been uploaded to the online service, it may not appear immediately.

What to do: Try again later and check the latest information through LESCO's official channels if the delay continues.

3. Temporary Website Problem

The online service may occasionally be unavailable because of maintenance, technical problems, or high traffic.

What to do: Refresh the page after a while or try opening it in another browser.

4. Internet or Browser Issue

A slow connection, outdated browser, or temporary browser problem can prevent the page from loading correctly.

What to do:

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Refresh the page.
  • Close and reopen your browser.
  • Try another browser.
  • Clear the browser cache if the problem continues.

5. Incorrect or Incomplete Information

If the service requests additional information, entering only part of the required details may prevent the bill from appearing.

What to do: Follow the instructions displayed on the bill portal and enter the requested information accurately.

If none of these steps works, contact LESCO through its official customer service channels.

Can You Check Your LESCO Bill Without a Reference Number?

The reference number is generally the most convenient way to check a LESCO bill online.

If you do not have it, first try to locate an older bill or a saved digital copy. You may also ask the person who manages the electricity connection to provide the reference number.

If neither option is available, contact LESCO and ask about the official process for identifying your connection.

You may be asked to provide consumer details or complete a verification process. Do not assume that a name, address, or phone number alone will be sufficient to retrieve the bill.

Tips for Safe and Accurate Online Bill Checking

Keep these points in mind when checking your electricity bill online:

  • Use the official LESCO website or the recognized PITC bill service.
  • Check the website address before entering your reference number.
  • Enter the complete reference number carefully.
  • Review the billing month before downloading or printing the bill.
  • Confirm the payable amount and due date from the current bill.
  • Avoid websites that request unnecessary personal or financial information.
  • Do not share your bill or consumer details publicly.
  • Keep a PDF copy if you need the bill for future reference.
  • Contact LESCO through official channels if the online information appears incorrect.

Remember: A duplicate bill is a copy of the billing document. It does not, by itself, confirm that the bill has been paid. Check payment status through the relevant official payment channel or your payment receipt.

Conclusion

Checking your LESCO bill online is a convenient way to view your electricity bill without waiting for a printed copy. In most cases, you can use your reference number to access the available bill through the online LESCO bill service.

With the correct reference number and access to the online service, you can manage the process from your phone or computer.
