How To Check GEPCO Bill Online – Complete Guide

January 1, 2025 GEPCO 10 min read

Checking your GEPCO electricity bill online is a convenient way to view your monthly electricity charges without waiting for a printed bill. Consumers can use the online billing service to access their available electricity bill, check the payment due date, review monthly consumption, and download a duplicate copy.

The Gujranwala Electric Power Company (GEPCO) provides electricity distribution services to its designated service areas in Pakistan. Consumers can generally retrieve their billing information through the online billing portal using the reference number printed on a previous electricity bill.

In this guide, you will learn how to check your GEPCO bill online, find your reference number, download a duplicate bill, and understand the information displayed on your electricity bill.

Quick Tip: Keep your previous GEPCO electricity bill handy — it contains your reference number required to check your bill online.

How to Check GEPCO Bill Online

You can check your GEPCO electricity bill online by using your electricity connection's reference number. You generally do not need to visit a GEPCO customer service office simply to view an available online bill.

Follow these steps to check your latest GEPCO bill.

Step 1: Open the GEPCO Online Bill Portal

First, open the official GEPCO online billing service.

GEPCO duplicate bill information is commonly provided through the Power Information Technology Company (PITC) billing system.

You can access the GEPCO bill portal at: https://bill.pitc.com.pk/gepcobill

Before entering your information, make sure you are using the correct GEPCO billing page and that the website is accessible.

Step 2: Find Your GEPCO Reference Number

Locate the reference number printed on your previous GEPCO electricity bill.

The reference number is an important identifier used to retrieve your electricity connection's billing information.

Many Pakistani electricity billing systems use a 14-digit reference number. Check your own GEPCO bill to confirm the exact number and format required.

Enter the number carefully because an incorrect digit can prevent the system from finding your bill.

Step 3: Enter Your Reference Number

Once the GEPCO online billing page opens, locate the reference number field.

Enter the required information exactly as displayed on your previous electricity bill.

Follow any instructions provided by the portal regarding spaces, formatting, or other required details.

Step 4: Submit Your Information

After entering your reference number, select the relevant button to submit your information.

The billing system will attempt to retrieve the electricity bill associated with your connection.

If the information is correct and the bill is available, the system should display the corresponding billing record.

Step 5: View Your GEPCO Electricity Bill

Once your bill appears, review the displayed information.

Depending on the available billing record, you may be able to see:

  • Consumer name and connection details
  • Reference number
  • Billing month
  • Meter reading
  • Electricity units consumed
  • Current electricity charges
  • Previous outstanding balance
  • Total payable amount
  • Payment due date

Check the consumer and connection information to ensure that the displayed bill belongs to your electricity connection.

How to Find Your GEPCO Reference Number

Your GEPCO reference number is required to identify your electricity connection when checking your bill online.

If you do not know your reference number, you can find it on a previous GEPCO electricity bill.

Look near the upper section of your printed bill, where customer and connection information is displayed.

The reference number is generally presented as a series of digits and may be divided into groups for readability.

Do not confuse your reference number with other information, such as:

  • Consumer name
  • Meter number
  • Account information
  • Billing month
  • Meter reading

Use the exact identifier requested by the online billing portal.

If you have lost your previous electricity bill and cannot locate your reference number, contact GEPCO through its official customer service channels to obtain the correct connection information.

How to Download GEPCO Duplicate Bill

A GEPCO duplicate bill is an electronic copy of your electricity bill that you can view, save, or print.

It can be useful if:

  • You have not received your printed electricity bill.
  • Your original bill has been misplaced.
  • You need another copy for your records.
  • You want to review your monthly charges.
  • You need to check the payment due date.

Follow these steps to download your duplicate bill:

  1. Open the GEPCO online bill portal.
  2. Enter your electricity connection's reference number.
  3. Submit the required information.
  4. Wait for your available bill to appear.
  5. Open the displayed bill.
  6. Select the download or print option if available.
  7. Save the document as a PDF on your device.

The available options may depend on how the billing portal displays the document.

If there is no direct PDF download button, you may be able to use your browser's print option and select Save as PDF.

This allows you to keep a digital copy of your available GEPCO electricity bill.

How to Print Your GEPCO Electricity Bill

If you need a physical copy of your electricity bill, you can print the available online document.

After retrieving your bill:

  1. Open the displayed electricity bill.
  2. Select the Print option, if available.
  3. Choose your connected printer.
  4. Review the page layout.
  5. Print the document.

If you do not have a printer, save the bill as a PDF and print it later from another device or printing service.

Make sure the reference number, billing information, and payment details are readable on the printed copy.

How to Check GEPCO Bill Due Date

Your electricity bill due date indicates the deadline for paying the amount shown under the bill's payment terms.

You can generally find this information on your latest GEPCO electricity bill.

After opening your online bill, look for the due date or last date of payment section.

You should also review:

  • Billing month
  • Total current charges
  • Previous outstanding balance
  • Total amount payable
  • Any applicable payment instructions

Always check the latest available bill because payment terms and amounts may change after the original billing date.

Checking the due date regularly can help you plan your electricity bill payment and avoid overlooking the payment deadline.

What Information Is Available on a GEPCO Electricity Bill?

A GEPCO electricity bill contains several details about your electricity connection, consumption, and monthly charges.

Understanding these details can help you review your electricity usage and identify billing information that may need clarification.

1. Consumer Information

This section contains information identifying the electricity connection. It may include the consumer's name, address, reference number, and other connection-related details.

Review this information to make sure you are checking the correct electricity bill.

2. Reference Number

The reference number is an important connection identifier used by the online billing system. It helps the system locate the billing record associated with your electricity connection.

3. Billing Month

The billing month identifies the period for which the electricity charges have been calculated. It helps you distinguish your latest electricity bill from previous billing records.

4. Meter Reading

The meter reading section contains information about the electricity meter readings used to calculate your consumption. Depending on the bill format, it may show previous and current meter readings.

5. Electricity Units Consumed

Electricity consumption is generally measured in kilowatt-hours (kWh), commonly called electricity units. The recorded consumption is used to calculate electricity charges according to the applicable tariff and billing rules.

6. Current Charges

Current charges represent the charges calculated for the billing period. These may include electricity consumption charges and other applicable billing components.

7. Previous Balance

If an earlier bill remains unpaid, its outstanding amount may appear as a previous balance.

If you have already paid your previous bill but the amount still appears, check your payment confirmation and contact the relevant payment provider or GEPCO for assistance.

8. Due Date

The due date shows the payment deadline displayed on your bill. Review this information to understand when the payment should be made.

9. Total Amount Payable

This section displays the amount payable according to the bill's calculation and payment terms.

Review the latest bill before making a payment, especially if an earlier amount remains outstanding.

Why Is Your GEPCO Bill Not Showing Online?

Sometimes your GEPCO electricity bill may not appear after entering your reference number. Several reasons can explain this problem.

Incorrect Reference Number

One of the most common reasons is entering an incorrect reference number. Check the number on your previous bill and make sure all digits have been entered correctly.

Wrong Billing Portal

Make sure you are using the GEPCO billing service rather than another electricity distribution company's portal. Different electricity providers maintain separate billing records.

Latest Bill Is Not Available Yet

Your latest bill may not appear if the new billing information has not yet been uploaded or updated in the online system. If your bill is unavailable, try checking again later.

Temporary Website Problems

The billing website may occasionally experience technical issues, maintenance, or temporary service interruptions. Wait for some time and try opening the portal again.

Incorrect Number Format

Some billing portals require the reference number to be entered in a particular format. Follow the instructions displayed on the website and avoid adding unnecessary spaces or characters.

Internet or Browser Problems

A weak internet connection or temporary browser issue can prevent the billing page from loading correctly. Try refreshing the page, checking your internet connection, or accessing the portal through another browser.

If the issue continues, contact GEPCO through its official customer support channels.

Can You Check Your GEPCO Bill Without a Reference Number?

Most online bill-checking processes require a reference number or another supported connection identifier.

If you do not have your reference number, try the following options:

  • Find an older GEPCO electricity bill.
  • Look for a previously downloaded duplicate bill.
  • Check whether your provider's billing service supports another identification method.
  • Contact GEPCO customer support.
  • Visit an authorized customer service office if you cannot retrieve the required information online.
Warning: Do not enter random numbers into the billing portal because the system may not be able to identify your electricity connection.

For accurate billing information, use the connection details provided by GEPCO.

Frequently Asked Questions About GEPCO Online Bill Checking

1. How can I check my GEPCO bill online?
Open the GEPCO online billing portal, enter the reference number printed on your previous electricity bill, and submit the required information. If your billing record is available, you can view your electricity bill online.
2. How can I check my GEPCO bill by reference number?
Visit the GEPCO billing portal and enter your reference number in the required field. Submit the information to retrieve the available electricity bill associated with your connection.
3. How can I download my GEPCO duplicate bill?
Enter your reference number on the GEPCO online billing portal. Once your bill appears, use the available download or print option to save a duplicate copy.
4. Is it possible to check a GEPCO bill online without a reference number?
The online portal generally requires a reference number or another supported connection identifier. If you do not have this information, contact GEPCO to identify your electricity connection.
5. How can I find my GEPCO reference number?
You can find your reference number on a previous GEPCO electricity bill. Look near the upper section of the bill where the connection and consumer information is displayed.
6. Can I check my GEPCO bill on my mobile phone?
Yes. You can generally access the online GEPCO billing portal through a mobile browser. Enter the required reference number to retrieve your available electricity bill.
7. How can I check my GEPCO bill due date?
Open your latest online GEPCO bill and locate the due date or last date of payment section. Review the payment information carefully.
8. Why is my GEPCO bill not showing online?
Your bill may not appear because of an incorrect reference number, temporary website problems, an unavailable latest billing record, or an issue with the internet connection.
9. Can I print my GEPCO online bill?
Yes. If the online bill is available, you can generally use the print option to obtain a physical copy. You may also save the document as a PDF and print it later.
10. Is checking a GEPCO bill online free?
Viewing an electricity bill through the official online billing service is generally free. However, payment services may have separate terms or charges.
11. Can I check my previous GEPCO electricity bill?
Availability of previous billing records depends on the billing portal and its record-retention features. If older bills are not displayed, contact GEPCO for assistance.
12. Is a GEPCO duplicate bill the same as the original bill?
A duplicate bill is an electronic copy of the billing record. It contains the available billing information, although specific requirements for printed copies or official documentation may vary.

Conclusion

Checking your GEPCO bill online is a convenient way to access your electricity billing information without depending entirely on a printed bill.

By using the GEPCO online billing portal and entering your correct reference number, you can view your available electricity bill, review monthly charges, check the due date, and download a duplicate copy.

Keep your reference number accessible and always use the appropriate official billing service to retrieve your electricity information.
