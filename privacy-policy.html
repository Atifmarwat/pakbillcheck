Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 Your Privacy Matters

At PakBillCheck.com, accessible at https://pakbillcheck.com/, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you share while using our website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, how cookies and third-party advertising services work, and what choices you have regarding your personal information.

By accessing or using PakBillCheck.com, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We May Collect

We may collect certain information when you visit or use our website.

Information You Provide

When using our bill-checking services, you may enter information such as:

  • Electricity bill reference number.
  • Customer ID or other information required to retrieve a bill.
  • Information you provide when contacting us.

Please avoid submitting unnecessary personal or financial information through our website.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit PakBillCheck.com, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device type and operating system.
  • Pages visited and time spent on the website.
  • Referring website or page.
  • Date and time of your visit.

This information may be collected through server logs, analytics tools, cookies, or similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through our website may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain our website services.
  • Help users access available electricity bill information.
  • Process information entered into bill-checking features.
  • Improve website functionality and user experience.
  • Understand website traffic and visitor activity.
  • Identify technical problems and prevent misuse.
  • Respond to user questions and support requests.
  • Display relevant advertisements where applicable.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

We do not intend to collect more information than is reasonably necessary for these purposes.

3. Electricity Bill Information

PakBillCheck.com may allow visitors to enter an electricity bill reference number or other required consumer information to access available bill details.

The information entered may be processed to provide the requested result.

Users should understand that electricity bill information may contain personal or account-related details. Only enter information that you are authorized to use.

Important: We do not ask users to provide banking passwords, ATM PINs, or payment card security codes to check an electricity bill.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

PakBillCheck.com may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website performance and understand how visitors use our services.

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.

They may be used for purposes such as:

  • Remembering certain website preferences.
  • Supporting website functionality.
  • Understanding visitor activity.
  • Measuring website performance.
  • Supporting advertising and ad personalization.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect how some website features work.

5. Third-Party Advertising and Google Ads

PakBillCheck.com may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners, including Google.

These advertising providers may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites. This information may be used to display advertisements that are relevant to your interests.

Google's Advertising Cookies

Google may use advertising cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites.

Google and its advertising partners may use information such as browser activity, device information, and browsing interactions to provide, measure, and improve advertisements.

For more information about how Google uses information from websites that use its services, visit: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Managing Google Ad Personalization

Users can manage their Google advertising preferences through: https://adssettings.google.com/

You can also learn more about Google's advertising and privacy practices through its Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Third-Party Advertising Partners

Third-party advertisers may use their own cookies and tracking technologies. Their collection and use of information are governed by their respective privacy policies.

PakBillCheck.com does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party advertising providers.

6. Advertising Consent Requirements

Depending on your location and applicable privacy requirements, we may request your consent before using certain cookies or displaying personalized advertisements.

For visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google may require a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF) when applicable to the advertising services being used.

Where required, visitors will be provided with options to:

  • Accept applicable cookies and advertising technologies.
  • Reject non-essential cookies.
  • Manage their consent preferences.
  • Change or withdraw consent where supported.

We aim to respect applicable consent requirements and provide appropriate choices regarding advertising and tracking technologies.

7. Third-Party Services and External Links

Our website may use third-party services to support website functionality, analytics, advertising, or other operations.

These services may process certain information according to their own privacy policies.

PakBillCheck.com may also contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy policies, content, or security practices of websites operated by other organizations.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of external websites before providing personal information.

8. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not intend to sell visitors' personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or improve our website.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or a valid request from an authorized government authority.

Third-party advertising and analytics providers may independently collect information through their technologies, as explained in this policy.

9. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to help protect information handled through our website against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Actual retention periods may depend on the type of information and the services used.

10. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

These rights may include:

  • Requesting access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion of personal information where applicable.
  • Objecting to or restricting certain processing activities.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Managing cookie and advertising preferences.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the contact information provided below.

11. Children's Privacy

PakBillCheck.com is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children for advertising or other purposes.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through our website, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, advertising practices, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page, along with a revised effective date.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically to stay informed about how their information is handled.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, please contact us:

Website: https://pakbillcheck.com/

Email: support@billcheck.pk

We will make reasonable efforts to review and respond to privacy-related inquiries.

Thank you for visiting PakBillCheck.com. Your privacy matters to us, and we aim to provide a useful website experience while respecting your information and privacy choices.
