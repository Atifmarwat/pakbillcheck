Check Electricity Bill (FESCO)

It is convenient to check electricity bills online. By using the online service for checking FESCO bills, customers can view their latest FESCO online bill without receiving a physical copy. You can see how much you owe, the due date for payment, your electricity usage, and download or print a copy of the bill.

What is FESCO?

Faisalabad Electric Supply Company (FESCO) supplies electricity to many residential, commercial, industrial and other customers in Punjab, Pakistan.

How to Check Electricity Bill Online?

Enter your 14-digit reference number to view your electricity bill. The FESCO online bill check 14-digit process allows you to view important information on the bill, including how much you owe, the month of the bill, your meter readings, how many units you have used, taxes and the total amount owing.

How to Check FESCO Electricity Bills Online

  1. Go to the FESCO Bill Checker.
  2. Insert the 14-digit reference number from your last FESCO bill.
  3. Click the 'Check' button.
  4. If you enter the correct reference number, you will be able to see the bill.

Download / Print Bill

If available, you can download or print a copy of the electricity bill to store as a backup copy.

How to Find FESCO Reference Numbers

  1. Find your last electricity bill.
  2. Look towards the top of the bill.
  3. Find the field titled Reference No.
  4. Carefully write down the 14-digit number.
  5. Input this number into the online bill checking service.

FAQs

1. How can I check my FESCO bill online?

Input your 14-digit reference number to view the latest bill information.

2. How can I download a duplicate FESCO bill?

Input your reference number into the online bill checker, then select download/print.

3. Is it free to check my FESCO bill online?

Yes, generally there is no charge to view the electricity bill online.
